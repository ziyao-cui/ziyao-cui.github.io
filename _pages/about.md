Hello! I'm Ziyao (Richard) Cui, a first-year PhD in Operations Research at MIT, advised by <a href="https://www.mit.edu/~rahulmaz/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Rahul Mazumder</a>. My research focuses on the mechanics and applications of model compression, particularly knowledge distillation and pruning. I study how compression alters core model behaviors like privacy and uncertainty, and develop methods that leverage these compressed models to efficiently guide large-scale data selection.

Prior to MIT, I earned my Bachelor's degree from Duke University, double-majoring in Computer Science (AI/ML concentration) and Statistical Science (Data Science concentration), with a minor in Economics. During my time at Duke, I was advised by <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/jpei/jian-peis-homepage" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Jian Pei</a> and <a href="https://users.cs.duke.edu/~cynthia/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Cynthia Rudin</a>.
